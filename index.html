Project Details

EzPark

A modern peer-to-peer parking web app where drivers can find and book parking spaces. And Space owners can allot their available spots for booking.

About The Project

EzPark connects drivers directly with private parking space owners.

Drivers can find nearby parking, book a verified spot, and navigate to it.

Space owners can list their available parking spots and manage bookings.
